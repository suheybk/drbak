How long does the examination process take?

The initial examination usually lasts 30–40 minutes . This time may vary depending on the patient's history, test results, and any tests that may be performed.
What treatments are available?

Our clinic offers a wide range of treatments, primarily for migraine , neuropathic pain , epilepsy , glutathione therapy , ozone therapy , Myers cocktail , and neural therapy . For detailed information, please visit our Services page.
What are EEG and EMG tests used for?

    EEG (Electroencephalography): Measures the electrical activity of the brain and is used in the diagnosis of epilepsy and similar conditions.
    EMG (Electromyography): Evaluates the electrical responses of muscles and nerves, detecting neuropathy or muscle diseases.

In which cases are glutathione or ozone therapy recommended?

Glutathione and ozone therapies are recommended to support detoxification in the body, increase energy levels, and combat chronic fatigue and oxidative stress. Suitability is determined by a doctor's assessment.

Is any preparation necessary for EEG/EMG/PSG tests?

Generally, for an EEG, it is recommended that hair be clean and oil-free, and that hairspray or gel not be used. For an EMG, the area to be tested should be exposed, and comfortable clothing should be preferred. For a PSG (sleep study), it is helpful to stick to your usual sleep routine and wear comfortable clothing. It is important to follow the specific preparation instructions given to you.
How is an online (telehealth) consultation conducted?

Online consultations are generally conducted via secure video call. You join the consultation by clicking on a link sent via email at the time of your appointment. It is recommended that you share your current medication list and any test results before the consultation.
How does the prescription and drug writing process work?

Medications deemed necessary after examination are prescribed. E-prescriptions can also be issued during online consultations if deemed appropriate. A doctor's evaluation is required before any prescription requests are processed.
Is a medical report or certificate of incapacity for work being issued?

These types of documents require inspection and completion of necessary examinations.
How is privacy and the protection of patient information ensured?

All patient information is kept confidential and shared only with necessary medical personnel. Electronic records are stored on secure servers; please review our Privacy Policy page for detailed information.
How long does it take to receive test results after an appointment?

The time it takes to get results varies depending on the type of test. Simple blood tests generally take 24–72 hours, EEG/EMG reports within a few days, while specialized laboratory or external tests may take longer. You will be informed of the exact timeframe at your appointment.
What should I pay attention to when coming to the clinic?

Arrive 10-15 minutes before your appointment; bring a list of your regularly used medications and any previous test results and reports. Also, choose comfortable and easily accessible clothing (especially if you are having an EMG or physical examination).
What about disabled access and parking?

Our practice has been designed with accessibility standards in mind. Limited parking is available nearby; those wishing to arrive by public transport or taxi can obtain information from our contact line.
