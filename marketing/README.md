## How to read these files

- TR is the canonical voice. EN is fully written. **AR/FR/ES are first-pass templates flagged for medical-translator review** per `memory/dr-bak-locked-decisions.md` §12.14. Do not publish AR/FR/ES copy without a medical translator's pass.
- All copy obeys TR Sağlık Bakanlığı 2018 Tanıtım Yönetmeliği and Tıbbi Deontoloji Tüzüğü §29: **no superlatives, no comparative claims, no fee-listing, no guaranteed-outcome language, no patient-name testimonials without recorded açık rıza**.
- "Words we never use" enforcement (from `docs/discovery.md` §3): *mucize, garantili, %100 başarı, Türkiye'nin en iyisi, son teknoloji*. The brand-voice skill flags these on lint.
- Voice dials: 60% formal, 50/50 clinical/warm, 60% authoritative, 80% restrained, 60% hopeful.
