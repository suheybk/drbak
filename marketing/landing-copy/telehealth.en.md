# Telehealth (Online Consultation)

**Follow-up after a first visit, medication dose adjustment, reviewing test
results together — when you don't need to come to clinic in person.**

## What telehealth is good for

- **Follow-up after the first visit** — to talk about how treatment is going
- **Medication dose adjustment** or side-effect review
- **Reviewing test results** together (you can share images on screen)
- **Prescription renewal** via the e-prescription system
- **Remote second opinion** — reviewing a treatment plan from another
  physician

## What telehealth is not for

- **First neurological exam** — a full neurological exam requires physical
  contact; the first assessment is done in person.
- **Emergencies** — for symptoms suggesting stroke (sudden weakness,
  speech disturbance, altered consciousness), call 112 immediately.
- **New-onset seizures** — physical exam and emergency-department
  assessment are needed.

## How a session works

1. You book; the join link arrives by email and SMS.
2. At your appointment time, click the link in your email.
3. It opens in your browser (Chrome / Safari / Firefox); no app install
   needed.
4. Session length is 15 minutes (follow-up).
5. If needed, an e-prescription is sent; any test orders are uploaded to
   your account.

## KVKK + GDPR

- The session runs over an **end-to-end encrypted** connection.
- Sessions are **not recorded** by default — recording happens only with
  the patient's explicit consent and a medical justification.
- Join links are **HMAC-signed** and expire after the appointment ends.
- Your health data is stored **within the EU** (Frankfurt).

## Preparation

- A good internet connection (Wi-Fi ideal)
- A quiet room; headphones if you have them
- Your medication list, latest test results, and any questions ready

## FAQ

**Can my first visit be online too?**
General rule: the first neurology visit is in person. In exceptional cases
(distant city, bedbound patient) the assessment can begin online; we plan
next steps together.

**Are e-prescriptions valid internationally?**
Within Turkey, e-prescriptions are accepted at any pharmacy. For
international patients, a PDF prescription is issued; your country's
prescription rules apply.

**Are sessions recorded?**
No, not by default. If a session needs to be recorded for medical-record
purposes, explicit consent is captured first.

**Which devices does it work on?**
Any device with a modern browser (phone, tablet, computer).
