# Neurology Consultation

**28 years of neurology practice with sub-specialty in pain medicine; a
consultation that listens to your history, not just your symptom.**

## When is a neurology consultation appropriate?

- Recurring or severe **headaches**, migraine attacks
- **Dizziness**, balance disorders, vertigo
- **Sleep disorders**: difficulty falling asleep, night-time awakenings,
  daytime sleepiness, suspected sleep apnea
- **Memory and attention problems**, suspected dementia
- **Tremor**, involuntary movements, loss of coordination
- **Fainting, loss of consciousness**, history of seizure
- **Numbness, tingling, weakness** in arms or legs
- **Nerve-origin pain** in face, back, neck, or chest (sub-specialty in
  pain medicine — algoloji)

## What happens at the first consultation

The first consultation runs 30–40 minutes. We cover:

1. **Detailed history** — present complaint, when it started, what makes
   it better or worse, family history.
2. **Medication and treatment history** — current medications, prior
   treatments tried, allergies.
3. **Neurological exam** — reflexes, muscle strength, sensation, balance,
   coordination, eye movements, cranial nerves.
4. **Plan for next steps** — investigations as needed (EEG, EMG,
   polysomnography, carotid Doppler), imaging if indicated, treatment
   framework.

You leave with a written summary or one uploaded to your account. If a
prescription is needed it is transmitted electronically.

## A holistic approach

The symptom itself matters less than **what the symptom means for you**.
The same headache can require three different plans in three different
patients; that's why the first visit aims to understand your process
rather than rush to a prescription.

## Preparation

Useful to bring:
- Reports of any prior MRI, CT, EEG, EMG, or blood tests (phone photos
  are fine)
- A list of current medications, with dosages
- Previous physicians' notes, discharge summaries if any
- A symptom diary (pain or dizziness: frequency, triggers, severity)
