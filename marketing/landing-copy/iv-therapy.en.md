# IV Therapy

**IV (intravenous) protocols, after medical assessment, in patients for
whom they are appropriate — without overstatement.**

> **An honest opening:** IV therapy is sold online as if it had an
> unlimited indication list. We don't run it as a "wellness" product. In
> our neurology and pain-medicine practice, IV protocols have a specific
> place — and this page explains where they belong, and where they don't.

## Protocols we offer

### Myers Cocktail
B-complex vitamins, vitamin C, calcium, and magnesium. Clinical use:
supportive in chronic fatigue, fibromyalgia, migraine prodromes. Note that
it is not a stand-alone treatment but part of an existing plan.

### High-dose vitamin C
Used in specific clinical situations as supportive therapy. **Pre-screening
blood tests are required** (G6PD deficiency contraindicates high-dose IV
vitamin C).

### Glutathione IV
A major component of the antioxidant system. Clinical uses include
support in neurodegenerative conditions, certain neuropathic-pain
protocols, and reduction of toxic load. (Long read:
[our article on glutathione](/en/blog/glutatyon-iv).)

### EBOO (multi-pass ozone)
A modality of ozone therapy used in conditions such as peripheral
vascular insufficiency, diabetic foot, and chronic fatigue. Detailed
indication assessment by a sub-specialist is required.

## Who it's for, and isn't

We always do this in order:

1. **Detailed history and physician assessment.** An IV protocol is chosen
   for a person, not for a symptom alone.
2. **Necessary blood tests.** G6PD before high-dose C; specific parameters
   before ozone; renal/liver function for glutathione.
3. **Consent.** Every IV protocol begins with a written information +
   consent form.

**Not appropriate for:**
- Pregnancy (in most protocols)
- Active severe infection
- Significant renal impairment (assessed per protocol)
- G6PD deficiency — contraindicated for high-dose vitamin C

## What a session feels like

- An IV line is placed once; sessions run 30–90 minutes depending on
  protocol.
- The IV fluid is delivered at a specific rate.
- Mild dizziness, warmth, or local tenderness are common.
- A short rest before standing is advised.

## FAQ

**How many sessions are needed?**
Depends on protocol. Most protocols run as 4–6-session courses.

**Will I feel side-effects?**
Mild dizziness or warmth is possible and usually settles within minutes.
Serious side-effects are rare; risk is low under physician supervision.

**Do you offer wellness IVs?**
No. We don't run IV protocols outside a clinical indication and a
physician assessment.
