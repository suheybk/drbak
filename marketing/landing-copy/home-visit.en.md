# Home Neurology Visits

**A full neurology assessment in your own home — for patients who can't
easily come to clinic.**

## Who this is for

- **Patients with mobility limitations**, bedbound, or wheelchair users
- **Older patients** for whom hospital travel is itself a strain
- **Post-stroke or post-neurological-event** patients in rehabilitation
- Patients **transitioning home from hospital** who need close follow-up
- **Pediatric neurology** assessments where home observation gives a
  truer picture (e.g. sleep observation)
- **Disabled** patients and people with limited mobility

## What can be done at home

- Full neurological exam (reflexes, strength, sensation, balance,
  coordination)
- Detailed history (the home setting often produces a more honest history)
- Prescription via the e-prescription system
- Tests: blood draws by visiting nurse; referrals for MRI and other
  imaging
- **At-home EEG** with a portable device
- **At-home sleep study** with a portable polysomnography device
- Treatment plan drawn up and explained with the family

## Logistics

- **Geographic coverage:** all of Istanbul's Asian side; selected
  European-side districts (confirmed at booking).
- **Duration:** home visits run 60–90 minutes.
- **Booking:** [online booking page](/en/book?service=home-visit) or
  WhatsApp +90 530 087 43 91.
- **Who comes:** the doctor, and a nurse if needed; family attendance
  is encouraged.

## Preparation

- A quiet room; a comfortable position for the patient
- Prior tests (reports or phone photos are fine)
- All current medications — original packaging, not just a list (so
  dosages can be verified)
- Family or caregiver present (the plan is discussed with them too)

## FAQ

**Is at-home EEG reliable?**
Yes. The portable EEG devices we use produce recordings comparable to
hospital standard. In some cases a home sleep-EEG combination gives more
information, because the patient sleeps in their own bed.

**Can a prescription be issued at home?**
Yes, via the e-prescription system.

**How is my home address protected under KVKK?**
Address data is stored encrypted; if no follow-up is needed it is removed
after the consultation (DSAR / erasure requests are always honoured).

**Are home visits suitable for emergencies?**
No — for acute neurological events, call 112 and go to the nearest
emergency department. Home visits are appropriate for follow-up after an
acute event.
